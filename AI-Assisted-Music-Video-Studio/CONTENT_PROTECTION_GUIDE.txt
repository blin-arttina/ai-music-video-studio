AI-ASSISTED MUSIC STUDIO AND VIDEO GENERATOR — CONTENT PROTECTION GUIDE
=========================================================================

This guide explains exactly what this app's protection features do,
how they work, and — just as important — what they do NOT do.

1. WATERMARKING (images and video)
------------------------------------
WHAT HAPPENS: When you export an image or video from the Media
Library, a visible text watermark (your name, project name, or custom
text) is burned into the file before it downloads. You can choose its
position (corner, center, tiled) and its opacity, but opacity is kept
between 25% and 85% — it can never be turned fully invisible, and it
can never be forced to fully obscure your work either. This is
required and cannot be switched off from Settings.

HOW: Images are watermarked with Pillow (a Python image library),
compositing the text directly into the pixel data. Video is
watermarked with ffmpeg's drawtext filter, which burns the text into
every frame so it stays attached for the full length of the video,
including if someone re-encodes or trims it later.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO: A watermark discourages someone from claiming
your work as their own or reusing it without credit. It does NOT make
copying technically impossible. Someone determined to remove a visible
watermark may be able to crop, blur, or paint over it. This app does
not claim otherwise.

2. TEXT OWNERSHIP PROTECTION (lyrics, notes, scripts, poems, captions)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT HAPPENS: Every time you save a piece of protected text, this app
automatically:
  - Computes a SHA-256 cryptographic hash of the exact text.
  - Timestamps the save.
  - Assigns or reuses a unique content ID for that item.
  - Increases the version number if the text changed.
  - Writes a permanent ownership record (creator, project, version,
    hash, timestamp) that is never edited or deleted by later saves —
    each edit adds a new record instead of changing an old one.
  - Keeps every previous version's full text in a revision history, so
    you can see exactly when something was created or changed, and
    restore an earlier version if you want to.

WHY THE HASH MATTERS: A hash is a fingerprint of your exact text. If
someone later shows you a copy of your lyrics, you (or this app) can
hash that copy and compare it to your registered hash — a match proves
it's identical to your registered version, without you having to make
your original text public to prove it.

EXPORTING WITH OWNERSHIP INFORMATION: When you export protected text,
the app appends an ownership block (content ID, creator, project,
version, hash, date) and, optionally, a copyright notice line.

3. THE IMPORTANT LIMIT OF ALL OF THIS
----------------------------------------
No software — this one included — can guarantee that someone else
won't copy text they're able to read, or won't try to crop a watermark
out of an image. What this app CAN do is combine several kinds of
evidence — ownership metadata, timestamps, version history,
cryptographic hashes, and clear copyright notices — into a record that
documents when you created something and that a given copy matches
what you registered.

THIS APP DOES NOT LEGALLY REGISTER OR GUARANTEE COPYRIGHT. Formal
copyright registration (for example, through a national copyright
office) and legal enforcement against infringement are separate legal
processes that this app does not perform. If you need formal
registration or plan to pursue legal action over your work, that
requires a real legal/registration service — this app's ownership
records can serve as supporting documentation for that process, not a
replacement for it.

4. CREATOR RESPONSIBILITIES
------------------------------
- Keep your Blind Art Server account credentials secure — ownership
  records are tied to your authenticated account.
- Decide deliberately what stays private vs. what you export or share;
  the app defaults new text to private, but you control that per item.
- Back up your projects regularly (Project tab → Create Backup / Export
  Complete Project) — ownership records live in this app's database,
  and a backup protects your work if something happens to the server.
- Remember that exporting a file is the moment protection is applied
  (watermark burned in, ownership block appended) — files copied
  directly out of the Media Library before export are not yet
  watermarked.
