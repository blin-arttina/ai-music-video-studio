What This App Does

This is an all-in-one studio for writing, recording, arranging, and exporting music and video — built so every control works with a keyboard and a screen reader, not just a mouse. There's no sign-in: opening this app's web address is what gives you access, and everything you create is saved automatically to this app's own project database. Below is a plain-language tour of every part of it, so you know it's here before you go looking for it.

Main Menu

Where you land when you open the app. From here you can start a brand-new project, import a project ZIP you exported earlier (on this device or any other), and see every project you've started so far. Everything else below lives inside a project, once you've created one.

Music & Text Studio

Write and save lyrics, notes, scripts, poems, captions, dialogue, or narration. Every time you save, it's fingerprinted, timestamped, versioned, and recorded in a permanent ownership history you can view or export with a copyright notice attached. While you're typing, your work is kept as a local draft automatically, so closing the tab or reloading the page by accident never loses it.

Media Library

Upload images, audio, music, video, voice recordings, animation files, or fonts — or record straight from your microphone into the project. Every audio, video, or image file gets a built-in player or preview. When you're ready to share a finished image or video, "Export With Watermark" adds a visible creator watermark (and optionally a hidden one) and lets you choose resolution, frame rate, and file format before downloading.

Library (Free Starter Content)

Ready-made project templates (like "Song with Vocals + Instrumental" or "Narrated Video") that instantly set up a Timeline for you, plus a few free original tones and background images you can drop straight into your Media Library. No account, payment, or third-party stock involved.

Timeline

Build a multi-track arrangement — add tracks for music, vocals, dialogue, sound effects, images, video, animation, or text, then add clips to them. Every clip is placed and trimmed using exact number fields (start time, duration, volume, fades) instead of dragging, so every value is readable and adjustable with a screen reader. Audio and video effects (EQ, reverb, crop, captions, crossfades, and more) are all here too, with Undo/Redo covering every change.

AI Assistant

Ask a question about the app or your project, get a draft set of lyrics or a video/scene plan to start from, or generate real spoken-word audio with AI Voice (a free, self-hosted voice model — no account or per-use cost). The AI always hands you a draft to edit, never a finished decision. AI Music, Image, and Animation generation show plainly whether a generation provider is connected on the server — never a faked result if one isn't.

Project Tools

Back up or fully export a project as a ZIP (this is also how you keep your own copy on your device, Google Drive, Dropbox, or Google One — you choose where it goes), duplicate or rename a project, keep a running audit log, or delete a project permanently. Since this app has no separate logins, anyone with the app's web address already has full access to every project — treat that address like a password.

Settings

Toggle whether a project is private, and save a studio name or logo once so it's used automatically as your watermark across every project, current and future. Watermarking and text ownership protection are always on and can't be turned off, by design.

Accessibility

Dark background, large high-contrast text, large buttons, descriptive labels instead of icon-only controls, full keyboard operability, and a "Skip to main content" link on every page. If something here ever reads out of order or skips content for you, tell the person maintaining this app — that's a bug to fix, not something to work around.
